# The Star Treatment

A reusable prompt for ChatGPT Images 2.6 that turns any creator into the star of a rollout. Attach their photo, type their name, and the model researches them and builds a bratty, famous, rich, photorealistic image around who they actually are. Add a place (for example Koreatown) and it puts them in a real, specific spot there.

## How to use it

1. In ChatGPT, select a **Thinking** or **Pro** model. Only then can Images 2.6 search the web before it draws (Plus, Pro, and Business plans) [17]. In Instant mode it skips the research and works from what it already knows.
2. Attach a clear, recent photo of the creator: face fully visible, front or three-quarter view, decent light. Images 2.6 can also save the photo as a named reference, so later chats can use `@name` instead of a new upload [18].
3. Paste the template below and fill in the top lines. Only `STAR` is required.
4. Refine with one change per message (see Follow-ups). Check each result at full size for likeness, hands, and background lettering.

Example header:

```text
STAR: Valkyrae
PLACE: Koreatown, Los Angeles
FORMAT: YouTube thumbnail 16:9
OUTPUT: 1 image
TEXT:
```

## The template

```text
THE STAR TREATMENT

STAR: [creator name]
PLACE: [optional, e.g. Koreatown, Los Angeles. Blank = your call]
FORMAT: [YouTube thumbnail 16:9 (default) | rollout poster 4:5 | Shorts cover 9:16 | cover art 1:1]
OUTPUT: [1 image (default) | rollout set of 4 | one beat: tease, reveal, drop day, or aftermath]
TEXT: [optional, exact words in quotes. Blank = no text]

The attached photo shows the STAR. Anything not set above is your call: decide it from research on the STAR, never from defaults.

Make photorealistic imagery for the STAR's rollout, the music-industry-style campaign for their next big drop. Treat them as the most famous person alive right now, in their spoiled, untouchable star era: same person, much richer life. The energy is bratty, famous, and rich. Every frame must look like a real photograph someone actually took, not an AI poster.

1. RESEARCH FIRST
Search the web for the STAR before drawing and quietly build a star file:
- What they're known for, their niche, and their current era: latest look, milestones, what they're doing now.
- What fans recognize instantly: hair, glasses, tattoos, nails, makeup, silhouette, how they dress.
- Their taste and palette: colors, fashion, food, cities, cars, games, music.
- Their lore: catchphrases, inside jokes, iconic moments, recurring bits, fan nickname, pets, hometown, heritage.
Then choose one signature trait to keep or push further, and 2–3 easter eggs only real fans would catch (an object, a color, a number, a food, a pet), placed naturally in the scene, never as logos or written names.
Uniqueness test: if a choice could be swapped onto any other creator without anyone noticing, replace it. Skip your first idea; use the second or third, more specific one.
If you can't browse or find little, build from the photo, what you already know, and the PLACE.

2. IDENTITY LOCK
Use the photo for identity only. Don't copy its pose, outfit, lighting, background, colors, or framing.
Keep: face shape, eye shape and spacing, brows, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone and undertone, freckles, moles, marks, real age, real body type.
Never beautify, slim, de-age, smooth the skin, lighten or darken it, or whiten teeth, and never drift toward a generic AI-pretty face. Fans must recognize them at thumbnail size.
Reinvent freely: pose, expression, wardrobe, hair styling, makeup, angle, light, setting. If their hair is a known signature, keep it.
The STAR is the only real person in the frame. Everyone else is an anonymous stranger.

3. THE ENERGY
Show it through behavior and detail, not by piling up props. These are starting points; invent the STAR's own version.
- Bratty: spoiled pop-star/heiress attitude. Unbothered, cheeky, a little rude, fully in control. Deadpan, a smirk, or a real laugh at something off-camera, never a polite camera smile. Mid-eye-roll; sunglasses at night, lowered with one finger; looking past the lens like the photographer is beneath them; on the phone, ignoring everyone; feet up where they shouldn't be; sitting on the table instead of the chair; eating something messy in an immaculate outfit. Tasteful and non-sexual: the attitude lives in the face and body language.
- Star: the world revolves around them. A driver holding the door, a bodyguard's shoulder at the edge of frame, an assistant's arm carrying their bags, flashes going off, strangers doing double takes, phones raised in the distance, their own face on a bus-shelter ad behind them (image only). They are the sharpest thing in the frame.
- Rich: specific and careless, never cliché. Cream leather, cashmere, silk, faux fur, diamonds catching the light, crystal glassware, a matching luggage set, heavy shopping bags with ribbon handles, a black car idling at the curb; a bag worth more than the restaurant tossed on a sticky table, fine jewelry with sweatpants, couture next to street food. Three wealth signals at most, all logo-free.
Commit to ONE story moment: caught arriving or leaving, an absurdly luxe version of their everyday habit or content niche, being waited on while ignoring it, or a place clearly emptied out just for them.

4. PLACE
If PLACE is set, pick a real, specific spot inside it (a particular corner, restaurant, store, parking garage, hallway), not a themed backdrop. If the name could mean several places, choose the most iconic and commit. Get the real architecture, street furniture, cars, and materials right, and choose the time and weather on purpose (harsh noon, overcast, blue hour, 2 a.m. fluorescents). Signage uses the correct local language and script: legible where in focus, soft elsewhere, no gibberish, no invented brands. Locals and staff go about their business: real ages, real clothes, not models, mostly not looking at the camera.
If PLACE is blank, pick somewhere that means something to the STAR and make it just as specific.
Make them belong there: lit by the scene's own light sources (streetlights, neon, store fluorescents, grill flame, flash) with matching direction and color, real contact shadows, reflections in glass, chrome, and any wet pavement. They physically use the place: sit on it, lean on it, eat from it. Nothing looks pasted in.

5. CAMERA
Pick the one approach that fits the moment:
a) Paparazzi night flash: direct on-camera flash, hard falloff into darkness, slight light trails, caught mid-move, imperfect crop.
b) Long lens from across the street: telephoto compression, an out-of-focus foreground obstruction (a passing car, a pole, a stranger's shoulder).
c) Deadpan flash editorial: direct flash, a crisp hard shadow behind them, high-fashion irreverence.
d) Night film: tungsten-balanced 35mm look, halation around lights, visible grain, deep blacks.
e) Party digicam: 2000s compact-camera flash, a little noise, the friend's camera at the afterparty.
f) Campaign hero: medium-format clarity, one big soft light from the side, real shadow on the other side.
Whichever you pick, it's a real photograph: one plane of focus with natural optical falloff, grain or noise that suits the camera, highlights allowed to clip, true blacks, small real imperfections (a flyaway hair, a crease, a smudge on glass). Compose off-center and asymmetric, with tension; tilted or tight crops are fine when they serve the moment. Hands and jewelry must read cleanly; simplify a pose rather than tangle fingers.

6. COLOR
Neutral, accurate white balance: whites read white and skin matches the photo. Color comes from the light sources you can see and from the STAR's signature palette, placed deliberately in 1–3 spots (nails, the car, a bag, a sign) against restrained neutrals.

7. FORMAT
Follow the line for the chosen FORMAT:
- YouTube thumbnail 16:9: face large and readable at phone size, one focal point, strong separation from the background, nothing important in the bottom-right corner (the timestamp covers it).
- Rollout poster 4:5: leave clean negative space for a headline.
- Shorts cover 9:16: keep the face and key details out of the bottom fifth and off the right edge (app buttons cover them).
- Cover art 1:1: one iconic image that still reads when tiny.

8. TEXT
If TEXT is set, render it exactly once, spelled exactly as written, in type that fits the STAR's era (for example a tiny lowercase sans like a luxury caption, a stark low-res system font, or a condensed serif masthead), placed in negative space and never over the face. If TEXT is blank, add no text; only real signage that belongs to the place.

9. ROLLOUT SET
If OUTPUT is a rollout set, make four images from one era, keeping the wardrobe, palette, camera language, and easter-egg logic consistent:
1) Tease: no face. An extreme detail fans can decode: hands, nails, jewelry, a reflection in sunglasses, the back of their head through a car window.
2) Reveal: the hero image.
3) Drop day: the chaos. Flashes, crowds, security; they're unbothered.
4) Aftermath: 3 a.m., still in the full look, alone, doing something extremely normal, caught by one flash.
If OUTPUT names one beat, make only that image.

10. HARD NOs (the ChatGPT look)
- Color: yellow, amber, or sepia cast; golden hour by default; teal-and-orange grade; oversaturation; HDR; faded matte grade; overall glow, haze, or bloom.
- Skin and face: plastic, waxy, airbrushed, or poreless skin; beauty-filter finish; over-sharpened, crunchy pores; glossy sheen everywhere; perfect symmetry; doll eyes; identical catchlights; over-whitened teeth; windswept or every-strand-perfect hair.
- Pose and composition: dead-center symmetry; stiff front-facing stance; stock-photo smile; crossed-arms or hands-on-hips power pose; peace sign, thumbs up, or pointing at the camera; open-mouth shocked YouTube face.
- Light: god rays, lens flares, glowing rim-light halos, floating particles, sparkles, dust motes, bokeh orbs, random smoke or fog, neon-rain cyberpunk mood, flat even beauty lighting.
- Background: generic blurred city, empty streets, mannequin crowds, everyone staring at the lens, repeated faces, gibberish signs, real or invented logos, a luxury penthouse at sunset.
- Rich clichés: raining money, cash fans, gold bars, champagne spraying, thrones, crowns, gold-plated everything, posing on supercars or jet stairs, Dubai skylines.
- Graphics: gradients, glassmorphism, glowing outlines, drop shadows, 3D chrome or bevelled text, outlined yellow text, red arrows or circles, stickers, emojis, brushstroke banners, multiple fonts, watermarks, film borders, film-stock edge text, date stamps.
- Style drift: 3D render, Pixar, anime, painterly, concept art, fantasy, epic movie-poster look, everything sharp front to back.

AFTER THE IMAGE
Below the image, write three short lines: the moment you chose, the exact spot, and the easter eggs you hid. For a rollout set, also write a five-line ERA LOCK (palette, wardrobe, camera, recurring prop, easter eggs) I can paste into future prompts.
```

## Follow-ups

Send one at a time in the same chat.

| Goal | Message |
| --- | --- |
| Another take | Same STAR and PLACE. New story moment and a different camera from the list. Same identity lock and hard NOs. |
| Brattier | Push the attitude further: more unbothered, more spoiled. Change nothing else. |
| Richer, quieter | Make the wealth quieter and more specific: fewer props, better materials, more people serving them. Change nothing else. |
| New place | Same look and era. Move them to PLACE: [new place]. Same rules. |
| Fix the likeness | Match the photo more closely: [eyes / nose / jaw / skin tone / hairline]. Change only the face. |
| Less AI | Make it read more like a real photo: neutral white balance, more grain, no glow or sheen, a messier, more lived-in background. Change nothing else. |
| Continue an era | ERA LOCK: [paste]. Next image in this era: [beat or idea]. |
| GIF | Turn this into a looping GIF: [one small motion, e.g. they lower their sunglasses, glance at the lens, push them back up]. Camera still; end on the starting pose. |

## Using the API instead

- Use `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst` for finals (best detail and likeness) or `gpt-image-2.6-flare` for fast drafts, through `v1/images/edits` with the creator's photo as the input image.
- These models only run on the Images endpoints, so they can't browse [14]. Do the research step separately and paste a few facts after the name, e.g. `STAR: Valkyrae — [3–5 facts: signature look, colors, lore]`.
- Settings: `quality: "high"`, and leave `complexity` at its default (`high`), which suits prompts that leave design decisions open. Both sides of `size` must be multiples of 16: `1536x864` or `2048x1152` for 16:9, `1024x1280` for 4:5, `864x1536` for 9:16, `1024x1024` for 1:1.
- The API returns only images, so the notes and ERA LOCK under the image only appear in ChatGPT. For a rollout set, make one call per beat.

## What "the ChatGPT look" is

Researched November 2026. Each tell, and where the template counters it:

| Tell | Countered in |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow, amber, or sepia cast (the "piss filter"), plus warm golden-hour light nobody asked for [1][2][3] | Color, Hard NOs |
| Waxy, glossy skin, oversaturated color, faces "a little too attractive", windswept hair [4] | Identity lock, Hard NOs |
| Averaged, symmetrical faces and dead-center compositions [5] | Identity lock, Camera |
| Perfectly even lighting, heavy bokeh, zero grain, and the "cinematic, 8K, hyper-detailed, studio lighting" vocabulary that drags every output toward the same look [6] | Camera (the template never uses those words) |
| A face lit differently from its background; backgrounds that look pieced together [4] | Place |
| A newer 2.5/2.6 habit: over-emphasized micro-texture and maxed-out specular highlights [7] | Hard NOs (crunchy pores, sheen) |
| 2.6 slightly smooths and warms skin on edits, and fills background text with invented, gibberish brand names [8] | Identity lock, Place, Hard NOs |
| 2.6 adds a film border with "KODAK PORTRA 400" edge text when a prompt mentions film [9] | Hard NOs |
| Design slop: gradients, glassmorphism, glow, brushstroke banners, cluttered layouts [3][10] | Text, Hard NOs |
| Thumbnail clichés: the shocked face (on only 11% of face thumbnails across 1,043 channels with 100K+ subscribers), arrows, circles, clutter [11][12] | Format, Hard NOs |
| Imagery that "looks expensive but says nothing" [13]: money, gold, champagne, supercars, penthouses | Energy (rich = specific and careless), Hard NOs |
| Every subject getting the same treatment | Research (uniqueness test, skip the first idea) |

The structure follows OpenAI's GPT-Image-2.6 prompting guide [14]: labeled sections, the word "photorealistic", a defined role for the reference photo, exact text in quotes with a stated count, and the guide's own framing of a person placed in a scene as "a real photograph someone could have taken, not an overly enhanced or cinematic movie-poster image."

## Notes

- OpenAI asks you to confirm you have permission to use a real person's likeness [15], so get the creator's sign-off for rollouts. ChatGPT also blocks photorealistic images of public figures who are minors [16].
- Don't add "in the style of [photographer]". ChatGPT refuses style requests that name living artists [16]; the camera list describes the techniques instead.
